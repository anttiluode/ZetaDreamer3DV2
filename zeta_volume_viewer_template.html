WHAT YOU ARE SEEING
The RGB cloud comes from the model's original output. The nodal surfaces are places where Re(Z)=0 or Im(Z)=0. Filament mode renders thin sub-voxel line segments defined by simultaneous Re(Z)=Im(Z)=0. These are extracted from a piecewise-linear approximation of the trained field, not blurred occupied voxels.

A feature near a Riemann zero is not automatically caused by that zero. The red markers are references only.
